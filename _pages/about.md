Hi! I'm **Mohammad Saddam Mashuri.** Some people prefer to call me **Mohammad.** Some **Saddam.** Call me whichever you prefer!

I was born in 2001 in **Jakarta, Indonesia.** As I go through life, I dabbled in a few things, but I got interested and put more work on **programming, 3D, and guitar**. Other than that, maybe I spend some time playing and immersing myself in video games when the occasional free time drops by.

For a short summary of where I've been, I've studied **Computer Science** at **Universitas Indonesia (UI)** in 2019, then transferred to **The Australian National University (ANU)** in 2022 as part of the Computer Science double-degree program. Once I finished my studies at UI and ANU in 2025, I got a **contract work at [BNI Sekuritas](https://www.bnisekuritas.co.id/)** as a **backend developer.** At work, I usually develop APIs and maintaining the backend of the BIONS application with some database management.
